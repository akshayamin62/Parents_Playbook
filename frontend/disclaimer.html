1. General Information

The Parent's Playbook, titled “My Child Goes to the Best School. Isn't That Enough?”, is an educational and self-reflection resource developed to help parents better understand their children and make more informed decisions about education, personal development and career planning.

The information, exercises, worksheets and recommendations provided through this website, the playbook, workshops and interactive sessions are intended for educational guidance and awareness.

They should not be interpreted as guarantees of academic achievement, career success or particular educational outcomes.

2. Educational Guidance, Not Professional Diagnosis

The playbook includes reflective questions, parent awareness exercises, self-assessment activities and practical planning tools.

These are intended to encourage thoughtful conversations and improve parental understanding.

The playbook is not a standardised psychological assessment, clinical diagnostic instrument or scientifically validated career-prediction tool.

It does not replace professional psychological evaluation, medical advice, mental-health care or other specialised services when such support is appropriate.

Parents are encouraged to seek assistance from suitably qualified professionals whenever necessary.

3. Individual Differences and Parental Responsibility

Every child is unique in terms of interests, abilities, personality, learning preferences, circumstances and developmental needs.

The ideas and activities presented in the playbook may not apply equally to every child or family.

Parents and caregivers remain responsible for determining the suitability of any recommendation or activity for their child's age, maturity, wellbeing and individual circumstances.

The author encourages informed parental participation, respectful family communication and age-appropriate independence rather than imposing predetermined educational or career choices.

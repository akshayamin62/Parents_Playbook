The central idea

Choosing a good school is important. But schooling and planning a child's education are not quite the same thing. Children need space to discover their interests, practise decisions, learn from setbacks and develop confidence in their own voice.

This playbook is designed to turn those concerns into honest family conversations and practical next steps, without demanding that a child choose a career too early.

Common questions

A few things parents ask

Who is this playbook intended for?

It is written for parents and caregivers supporting school-going children, particularly those preparing for subject, stream and future education decisions. Activities can be adapted to the child's age and readiness.

Does the playbook predict my child’s career?

No. It is an educational reflection and family discussion tool, not a standardised psychological test, clinical diagnosis or career predictor.

Can I use it without attending a workshop?

Yes. The book is designed for independent use. The workshop offers guided discussion and help interpreting the exercises in everyday family life.

What happens in a one-to-one session?

It starts with your family's concerns and current decision-making challenges. The specific scope, duration and fee are discussed before an appointment is confirmed.

1. Introduction

Welcome to The Parent's Playbook.

We respect your privacy and are committed to handling your personal information responsibly, transparently and securely.

This Privacy Policy explains how personal information may be collected, used, stored and shared when you:

  • Visit our landing page or website.
  • Download the free preview of The Parent's Playbook.

  • Request a one-to-one education and career guidance session.
  • Contact us or voluntarily subscribe to communications.

By using our services, you acknowledge this Privacy Policy. Where consent is required, we will request it separately.

3. Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with our website and services, we may collect:

A. Personal and contact information

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Mobile or WhatsApp number, if requested
  • City or location, if voluntarily provided
  • Enquiry details and communication preferences

B. Educational information

When you voluntarily request personalised guidance, we may ask for information such as:

  • Your child's class or educational stage
  • Academic interests and educational aspirations
  • Career-planning concerns
  • Learning preferences and educational challenges
  • Information you choose to share during workshops or counselling sessions

We encourage parents not to provide unnecessary sensitive information or identifiable information about children through general website enquiry forms.

C. Purchase and registration information

When payment and booking services are enabled, we may collect transaction references, purchased services, registration details and information required for invoicing.

Payment-card information, UPI credentials and other payment details will be processed by the applicable payment service provider according to its own privacy and security practices.

D. Technical information

Our website or hosting provider may automatically process limited technical information such as IP addresses, browser types, device information, access times and server logs for website delivery, security and troubleshooting.

Additional analytics or tracking technologies will be described and consent obtained where required before they are enabled.

4. How We Use Your Information

We process personal information for relevant and clearly identified purposes, including:

  1. Responding to your enquiries and requests.
  2. Providing access to the digital playbook or free preview.
  3. Processing orders, payments and invoices.
  4. Managing workshop registrations and interactive sessions.
  5. Scheduling and conducting one-to-one consultations.
  6. Providing customer service and resolving complaints.
  7. Improving our educational content and website experience.
  8. Maintaining appropriate financial, administrative and legal records.
  9. Sending educational updates or promotional communications when you have provided the necessary consent.
  10. Protecting our website and services from misuse or unauthorised access.

We will not use your personal information for unrelated purposes without an appropriate lawful basis.

5. Free Preview Downloads

The free preview of The Parent's Playbook is intended to help parents understand the book's educational approach and practical value.

On this website, visitors request the free preview by providing name, email, mobile number and city. We email the PDF to the address provided and may notify our team of the request.

We clearly explain what information is requested and why at the point of collection.

Requesting the free preview does not automatically subscribe you to promotional emails, WhatsApp messages or marketing campaigns unless you separately opt in.

6. Workshops and One-to-One Sessions

Parents participating in workshops or individual sessions may voluntarily discuss their children's educational experiences, interests, concerns and future plans.

We use information shared during these sessions to provide the requested educational guidance.

We will not publicly share identifiable family discussions, assessment responses or personal experiences without appropriate permission or another lawful basis.

If any workshop or consultation is to be recorded, participants will be informed in advance, and any required consent will be obtained.

Our educational guidance does not constitute a clinical psychological diagnosis or a guarantee of academic admission or career success.

7. Children's Privacy

Our website and services are primarily intended for parents and adult caregivers.

We do not intentionally seek personal information directly from children through the general landing page.

Where information concerning a child is required for an educational service, we will seek appropriate parental or lawful guardian authorisation and follow applicable legal requirements.

We do not use children's personal information for behavioural advertising or unrelated commercial profiling.

Parents should avoid submitting unnecessary information about their children, including medical records, identity documents or private family circumstances, unless specifically required through an appropriately protected process.

We may invite visitors to receive:

  • Parenting and educational insights
  • Playbook-related updates
  • Workshop announcements
  • Information about education and career-planning services
  • Relevant newsletters or learning resources

Promotional communications will be sent only where permitted under applicable law and any required consent has been obtained.

9. Sharing of Personal Information

We do not sell personal information to third-party advertisers or data brokers.

Where necessary, we may share relevant information with authorised service providers, including:

  • Website hosting and technical support providers
  • Payment processing services
  • Email delivery and communication providers

  • Service providers supporting the delivery of purchased products
  • Professional advisers or authorities where disclosure is legally required

Service providers will receive only the information reasonably necessary for their assigned functions and will be subject to applicable contractual and legal obligations.

10. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures intended to protect personal information against unauthorised access, misuse, accidental loss, alteration or disclosure.

These measures may include access controls, secure hosting, encrypted transmission, appropriate authentication and restricted handling of personal information.

No online system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

If a personal data breach occurs, we will assess and respond to it and provide notifications where required by applicable law.

11. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected, provide requested services, meet legal obligations or resolve legitimate disputes.

Different categories of information may have different retention periods.

For example, transaction and invoicing records may need to be retained for statutory requirements, while routine enquiries may be deleted when they are no longer needed.

When personal information is no longer required, we will delete, anonymise or otherwise securely dispose of it, subject to applicable legal requirements.

However, website hosting and infrastructure services may process technical data necessary to deliver and protect the site.

If optional analytics, advertising pixels or other non-essential tracking technologies are introduced, this policy and any applicable consent mechanisms will be updated.

You may also manage cookies through your browser settings.
